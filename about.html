About FinLogic Hub

FinLogic Hub summarizes selected state filing deadlines, recurring fees, and official filing paths in a shorter format. The goal is simple: help you find the right state rule and the right official source faster.

Every live guide starts from an official state source and shows a visible review date. These pages are for planning only. If the state record or filing portal says something different, follow the official source.

How a guide is reviewed

  1. Identify the filing name, entity type, and timing rule on the responsible state agency's website.
  2. Cross-check the amount against an official fee table, form, or filing portal instruction.
  3. Separate the normal filing path from late, dissolved, revoked, or reinstatement status.
  4. Link the supporting official pages and record the date the guide was checked.

A guide's review badge changes after 30 days and is marked as needing a fresh review after 45 days. The date belongs to that individual guide; a recent date on one state does not mean every state page was reviewed on the same day.

Editorial rules for live pages

  • Use official state sources for deadlines, filing fees, and late-payment language.
  • Keep entity types separate when the state applies different rules.
  • Show the review date on the page.
  • Link back to the official filing page or statute.
  • Say "not clearly stated" when the official guidance does not publish a number.
  • Remove unsupported or outdated pages instead of keeping uncertain information live.

What this site does not do

FinLogic Hub is not a state agency, law firm, tax adviser, registered-agent service, or filing service. The site does not submit filings or collect state filing fees. It helps readers find the relevant rule and then continue on the official state website.

Corrections

State pages and fee schedules change. To report a mismatch, email feeligfeelig@gmail.com with the state, entity type, and official source link. The correction process starts by checking the cited state source rather than copying another private summary.
