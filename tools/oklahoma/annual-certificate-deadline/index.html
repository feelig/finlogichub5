Oklahoma

Oklahoma annual certificate fee and deadline

Use this page if you need Oklahoma's clearest recurring business-filing rule. Domestic and foreign Oklahoma LLCs file an annual certificate on the anniversary date of registration, and Oklahoma's tax agency says the old corporate franchise-tax filing requirement ended starting with tax year 2024.

Recently checked Checked: July 7, 2026 Reviewed by FinLogic Hub Editorial Team 5 official sources Source: Oklahoma Secretary of State and Oklahoma Tax Commission

At a glance

LLC due rule Domestic and foreign Oklahoma LLCs file each year on the anniversary date of registration.
LLC filing fee $25 annual certificate filing fee.
Late consequence An LLC that misses the annual certificate for 60 days after the due date ceases to be in good standing.

Oklahoma searches often mix the Secretary of State annual certificate with the old corporation franchise-tax filing. The tax agency now says franchise tax returns stopped after tax year 2023, so the recurring Oklahoma answer is cleaner than many older pages suggest.

Domestic or foreign LLC Use the annual-certificate lane

The Oklahoma Secretary of State's annual certificate form gives the cleanest current recurring rule: anniversary date, $25 fee, and a 60-day good-standing cutoff.

Corporation Do not rely on old franchise-tax pages

The Oklahoma Tax Commission says franchise tax filings ended starting with tax year 2024, so confirm whether you are looking at an old rule or a different current tax obligation.

Already late LLC Check good standing before you file

Once the annual certificate is more than 60 days late, the LLC stops being in good standing, so verify the live status before assuming the normal filing path is enough.
