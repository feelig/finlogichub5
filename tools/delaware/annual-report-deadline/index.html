Delaware

Delaware annual report deadline for corporations and LLC annual tax

If you searched Delaware annual report, start with the entity type. Domestic corporations file annual reports and franchise tax by March 1, foreign corporations file annual reports by June 30, and Delaware LLCs do not file annual reports because they pay a $300 annual tax by June 1.

At a glance

Domestic corporation Annual report and franchise tax due March 1.
Delaware LLC No annual report; $300 annual tax due June 1.
Foreign corporation Annual report due June 30 with a $125 filing fee.

Delaware annual report searches often mix corporation filing rules with the separate LLC annual tax. This page keeps those lanes separate so you do not pay or quote the wrong requirement.

Pick your case first

Start with the entity type that matches your record

Use the matching case below before you rely on a fee, filing path, or due date.

Domestic corporation Use the March 1 report and franchise-tax lane

Domestic corporations file the annual report and pay franchise tax by March 1, so start there if the record is a Delaware corporation.

Foreign corporation Use the June 30 annual-report lane

Foreign corporations still file an annual report, but the due date and fee lane differ from Delaware domestic corporations.

Delaware LLC, LP, or GP Do not look for an annual report

Delaware LLCs, LPs, and GPs use the separate annual-tax path instead of a corporation annual report, so confirm the June 1 tax lane first.
