Nebraska

Nebraska annual and biennial report fee and deadline

Use this page if you need the recurring Nebraska filing rule. Start with the entity type because Nebraska splits business corporations into an even-year March occupation-tax lane, places LLCs and nonprofits into an odd-year April biennial lane, and keeps LLPs on an annual April cycle.

At a glance

Business corporation lane Domestic and foreign business corporations file biennial occupation tax reports in even-numbered years by March 1, and the state marks them delinquent on April 15.
LLC common lane Domestic and foreign LLCs file biennial reports in odd-numbered years by April 1, and the Secretary of State's current warning says LLCs can file online for $28.
Late-state risk If the annual or biennial report is not filed by the delinquency date, the state can administratively dissolve or revoke the entity and push the work into reinstatement.

Pick your case first

Start with the entity type that matches your record

Use the matching case below before you rely on a fee, filing path, or due date.

Business corporation Corporations use the even-year March lane

Business and professional corporations use the biennial occupation tax report, not the April LLC calendar.

LLC or PLLC LLCs use the odd-year April biennial lane

Nebraska's current SOS warning highlights the online LLC biennial report at $28, which is the cleanest current public number for this lane.

Nonprofit corporation Nebraska nonprofits match the LLC due year

Domestic and foreign nonprofits use the odd-numbered-year April 1 biennial cycle rather than the even-year corporation lane.

LLP or already late LLPs stay annual and late records move to reinstatement

LLPs file every year by April 1, and once any Nebraska record crosses the delinquency date the state can move it into dissolution, revocation, or reinstatement work.

Question What due date should I start with for Nebraska?

Start with March 1 or April 1 depending on entity type, with separate annual-benefit-report timing for benefit corporations. If the state uses anniversary, report-month, or fiscal-year timing, the official state record still controls.

Question What fee or amount does this page show?

This page starts with Common $28 online Nebraska LLC biennial report lane, with separate $30 or reinstatement fee paths on other entity types. If the amount changes by entity type, filing method, or status, confirm it on the official state source before paying.

Question What if I am already late?

Delinquent June 16, then administrative dissolution or revocation if still unresolved. Confirm the current status and amount on the official state record before you pay or file.
