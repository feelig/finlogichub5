Rhode Island

Rhode Island annual report fee and deadline

Use this page if you need the Rhode Island annual report deadline or fee. Start with the filing period because Rhode Island opens the annual report from February 1 through May 1, then splits the price between the standard $50 business-entity lane and the lower $20 nonprofit lane.

At a glance

Main filing period Rhode Island annual reports use a February 1 through May 1 filing period.
Business-entity fee lane The current Rhode Island corporation and LLC annual report forms show a $50 filing fee.
If already late The current Rhode Island annual report forms say a $25 penalty fee applies when the report is not filed within the statutory period stated on the form.

Business corporation Corporations use the $50 annual report lane

The current Rhode Island business and foreign corporation annual report forms both show the February 1 to May 1 filing period with a $50 filing fee.

LLC LLCs share the same seasonal filing window

Rhode Island LLC annual report forms use the same February 1 to May 1 period as corporations and also show the $50 filing fee.

Nonprofit corporation Nonprofits still file annually, but the fee is lower

The current Rhode Island nonprofit annual report form shows the same filing period but uses the lower $20 filing fee.

Online filing Online filing can add an access fee that is separate from the state filing fee

Rhode Island's online filing help explains that enhanced access fees are charged by the portal vendor and are separate from the state's own filing fee.

Question What due date should I start with for Rhode Island?

The main due date on this page is February 1 through May 1. If the state uses anniversary, report-month, or fiscal-year timing, the official state record still controls.

Question What fee or amount does this page show?
