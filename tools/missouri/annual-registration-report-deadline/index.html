Missouri

Missouri annual registration report fee and deadline

Use this page if you need the Missouri annual registration report deadline. Missouri's cleanest split is for-profit corporation versus nonprofit corporation because the fee tables differ and nonprofits use the separate August 31 reporting rule.

Recently checked Checked: June 8, 2026 Reviewed by FinLogic Hub Editorial Team 5 official sources Source: Missouri Secretary of State

At a glance

For-profit corporation lane Common current fee lane is $20 online or $45 by paper, with the report due at the end of the incorporation or qualification month for most modern corporations.
Nonprofit lane Missouri nonprofits file by August 31 each year, commonly at $10 online or $15 by paper.
Late rule Late reports are subject to an additional $15 fee for each 30-day period.

Missouri's recurring filing is best modeled as a corporation report, not a generic all-entity annual report. The Secretary of State's current fee schedule and filing pages are especially useful here because they show the online-versus-paper price split clearly.

What to know first

What to check before you file in Missouri

Entity lane Due rule Published amount What to watch
For-profit corporation incorporated or qualified on or after July 1, 2003 Due at the end of the month in which the corporation incorporated or qualified $20 online or $45 paper Missouri highlights the online filing savings directly on its annual-report guidance.
Older corporation with a legacy report month Due at the end of the month shown on the last annual report $20 online or $45 paper Older corporations should not assume the incorporation month if the state already established a different report month.
Nonprofit corporation Due by August 31 each year $10 online or $15 paper Missouri's nonprofit instruction sheet says the corporation will not remain in good standing if the report is not filed.
Already late report File as soon as possible using the correct annual-report form or portal path $15 additional fee for each 30-day late period The late fee stacks by 30-day periods, and continued failure to file can move the record into administrative dissolution or revocation.

Quick questions

What most filers need to know first

These answers keep the page short while still covering the questions people ask before they file.

Question Who should use this Missouri guide?

Use this page for For-profit and nonprofit corporations using the Missouri registration-report system. Match the entity type on the official state record before you rely on the summary.

Question What due date should I start with for Missouri?

The main due date on this page is End of the incorporation or qualification month for many corporations, or August 31 for nonprofits. If the state uses anniversary, report-month, or fiscal-year timing, the official state record still controls.

Question What fee or amount does this page show?

The main amount on this page is $20 online or $45 paper for many for-profit corporations, or $10 online or $15 paper for nonprofits. If the amount changes by entity type, filing method, or status, confirm it on the official state source before paying.

Question What if I am already late?

$15 additional fee for each 30-day late period. Confirm the current status and amount on the official state record before you pay or file.
