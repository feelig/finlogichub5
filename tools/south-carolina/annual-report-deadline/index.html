South Carolina

South Carolina annual report and license fee deadline

Use this page if you need South Carolina's recurring corporation filing rule. South Carolina folds the annual report into the Department of Revenue filing, changes the due date with the tax year, and pairs the report with a corporate license fee rather than a flat Secretary of State annual-report price.

Recently checked Checked: June 8, 2026 Reviewed by FinLogic Hub Editorial Team 5 official sources Source: South Carolina Department of Revenue

At a glance

C corporation lane The annual report is included with SC1120 and is due April 15 for calendar-year filers or the 15th day of the fourth month after tax year end.
S corporation lane SC1120S is due March 15 for calendar-year filers or the 15th day of the third month after tax year end.
License fee rule South Carolina publishes a license fee of 0.1% of capital stock and paid-in surplus plus $15, with a $25 minimum.
