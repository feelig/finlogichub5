District of Columbia

District of Columbia biennial report fee and deadline

Use this page if you need the District of Columbia biennial report deadline. DLCP says domestic and foreign filing entities file the first report by April 1 of the next year after registration, then file again every two years after that.

Recently checked Checked: July 7, 2026 Reviewed by FinLogic Hub Editorial Team 7 official sources Source: District of Columbia Department of Licensing and Consumer Protection

At a glance

First report rule First reports are due April 1 of the very next year from the year of registration.
Recurring rule Subsequent reports are due April 1 every two years thereafter.
Published amount $300 biennial report fee plus a $100 late fee.

District filings are easy to misread because the first report arrives faster than many people expect. The District also warns that foreign entities may owe back reports if they started doing business before registering.

Pick your case first

Domestic filing entity Use the next-April-1 first-report rule

Domestic LLCs, corporations, nonprofits, LLPs, LPs, and other filing entities start with the next April 1 after registration.

Foreign filing entity Check for back-report exposure

DLCP says foreign entities might be liable for back reports if they commenced business before registration, so confirm the record history before paying.

Already late Expect the $100 late fee

The District publishes a $100 late fee on top of the $300 biennial report fee, so late filings should be priced from the late lane first.
